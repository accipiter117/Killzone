Trooper style check

Third pass. Each trooper is now about 70 pixels tall and built from dozens of shaped pieces of kit, each shaded in its own material and outlined, so the equipment reads clearly. Original art drawn in code; the references guided the kit, nothing is traced.

Helghast rifleman

Peaked helmet with rim band, crest and neck guard; gas mask with ringed glowing lenses, respirator, filter and hose; armoured vest with crossed straps and three pouches; shoulder armour and red armband; belt, buckle and pouches; long greatcoat split at the front; knee pads and strapped boots; rifle with a curved magazine, vented handguard, muzzle brake and glowing sight.
