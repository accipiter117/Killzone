Tank style check

One tank per side, drawn in code in the same style as the troopers. Same job and stats for both (so the battle stays fair), a distinct look for each faction. Approve the look here and the gameplay gets built next.

ISA tank

Low and angular: a long sloped wedge hull with side skirts over the tracks, a clean low turret with a sensor block and hatch, a smooth gun with a fume extractor and muzzle brake, a yellow unit marking and blue running lights.

Helghast tank

Tall and hunched: riveted charcoal plates with rust, exhaust stacks at the back, a row of glowing orange vents, a slatted front grille with two glowing eyes like the gas masks, a heavy mantlet and a long ringed cannon.
