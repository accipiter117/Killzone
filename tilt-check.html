Tilted view check

The same battlefield, same units, drawn two ways: as now, and tilted into a three quarter view so hills, ruins, trenches and craters stand up off the ground. Only the picture changes; the rules stay the same.

  • Raised ground lifts up the screen with a tall banded cliff face, and anyone standing on it is lifted with it.
  • Ruins become walls with height: lit tops, shaded fronts, window gaps, long shadows.
  • Trenches show their planked far wall and floor; craters are bowls.
  • A little more ground life (tyre ruts, grass tufts, pebbles) but still quiet so units read clearly.
